Breaking the Silence: AI-Powered Menopause Education System

Assignment Overview

Aspect Details
Summary Students design and develop a culturally-sensitive AI system for menopause education, addressing global health education gaps through personalized Large Language Model applications. The assignment combines health informatics, cultural sensitivity, AI ethics, and technical implementation.
Topics Large Language Models, AI Ethics, Cultural AI, Health Informatics, Bias Detection, Human-Computer Interaction, Natural Language Processing, AI Safety
Audience High School Students (Grades 9-12) and CS1 College Students
Difficulty Moderate to Advanced. Phase 1: 2-3 weeks (research/design). Phase 2: 3-4 weeks (technical implementation). Total: 5-7 weeks
Strengths • Addresses real-world health equity issues
• Combines technical skills with social impact
• Teaches AI ethics through practical application
• Culturally inclusive and globally relevant
• Integrates multiple AI concepts (LLMs, bias, safety)
Weaknesses • Requires sensitive handling of health topics
• May be challenging for students without health background
• Cultural research requires substantial time investment
• Technical implementation requires programming experience
Dependencies High School: Basic understanding of AI concepts, internet research skills, presentation abilities
CS1: Python programming fundamentals, basic web technologies (HTML/CSS), introduction to APIs, data structures
Variants Simplified: Focus only on Phase 1 research and design
Advanced: Implement machine learning models for bias detection
Cross-curricular: Partner with health/biology classes
Global: Compare multiple cultural contexts beyond India

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • AI Ethics: Understand bias, fairness, and cultural sensitivity in AI systems
  • Health Informatics: Apply AI to address real health education challenges
  • Cultural Competency: Design technology that respects diverse cultural perspectives
  • Technical Skills: Implement basic AI safety and validation mechanisms
  • Research Skills: Conduct primary research on user needs and cultural contexts
  • Critical Thinking: Evaluate AI capabilities and limitations in sensitive contexts

📋 Phase 1: Research and Design (Weeks 1-3)

Objective: Understand the global menopause education crisis and design a culturally-sensitive AI solution

Step 1: Problem Analysis (Week 1)

Research Tasks:

  1. Global Health Gap Analysis
    • Research menopause education statistics globally
    • Identify cultural variations in menopause perception
    • Document economic and social impacts
    • Create infographic summarizing key statistics
  2. Cultural Deep Dive
    • Choose 2-3 cultures to compare (must include India)
    • Research "liberation vs. loss" perspectives
    • Interview 2-3 women from different cultural backgrounds (with consent)
    • Document linguistic and social barriers
  3. Current Solution Analysis
    • Evaluate existing menopause education resources
    • Identify gaps and limitations
    • Research AI health education applications

Step 2: User Research (Week 2)

Create User Personas:

  1. Persona 1: Rural Indian woman, 45-50 years, limited education, views menopause as liberation
  2. Persona 2: Urban professional woman, 48-52 years, college-educated, experiences menopause anxiety
  3. Persona 3: Western woman, 50-55 years, tech-savvy, seeking medical solutions
  4. Persona 4: Young woman, 25-30 years, wants to prepare for future menopause

Design Survey (10-15 questions):

  • Menopause knowledge assessment
  • Cultural attitude measurement
  • Information-seeking preferences
  • Technology comfort levels
  • Language preferences

Conduct Primary Research:

  • Deploy survey to at least 20 respondents
  • Ensure diverse demographic representation
  • Analyze results and identify patterns

Step 3: System Design (Week 3)

Architecture Design:

  1. Cultural Assessment Module: Design screening questions to identify cultural perspectives
  2. Content Pathway Logic: Define rules for routing users to appropriate content paths
  3. Safety Mechanisms: Plan human oversight and medical accuracy validation
  4. Bias Prevention: Design strategies to detect and mitigate cultural bias

Deliverables for Phase 1:

  • Research report (8-10 pages) with citations
  • User persona profiles with supporting evidence
  • Survey results analysis with visualizations
  • System architecture diagram with detailed explanations
  • Ethical considerations document
  • Implementation proposal for Phase 2

👨‍🏫 Instructor Notes

Preparation Requirements:

  • Sensitivity Training: Instructors should familiarize themselves with menopause health topics and cultural sensitivities
  • Ethics Review: Ensure compliance with school/university ethics policies for student research
  • Technical Setup: Verify Python environment, API access for LLM services, and necessary libraries
  • Cultural Consultation: Consider involving cultural studies or anthropology faculty as consultants
  • Medical Review: Arrange access to healthcare professional for accuracy review of student work

Adaptation Suggestions:

  • High School Simplified: Focus more on Phase 1 research, use no-code tools for prototyping
  • CS1 Enhanced: Include machine learning components, more sophisticated bias detection
  • International: Adapt cultural focus to local contexts while maintaining India comparison
  • Interdisciplinary: Partner with health education, sociology, or women's studies courses
  • Extended Timeline: Can be expanded to full semester project with deeper technical implementation

Assessment Modifications

  • Peer Review Component: Students review each other's cultural sensitivity approaches
  • Expert Guest Evaluation: Invite menopause specialists or AI ethicists to evaluate final presentations
  • Community Impact Assessment: Evaluate potential real-world impact and scalability of solutions
  • Reflection Essays: Students write about what they learned about AI ethics and cultural sensitivity
