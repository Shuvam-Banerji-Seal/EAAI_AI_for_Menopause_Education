## 🌍 Educational Impact

### Addressing the Global Health Crisis
This assignment tackles a real-world problem with significant societal impact:
- **Global Scope**: Over 1 billion women worldwide are affected by menopause
- **Educational Gap**: 90-94% of women receive no formal menopause education
- **Economic Impact**: $2-12 billion annual burden from untreated symptoms
- **Cultural Barriers**: Particularly acute in diverse contexts like India, where women experience menopause 4-5 years earlier

### AI for Social Good
Students learn to apply AI technology ethically and responsibly:
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Designing AI that respects diverse perspectives
- **Bias Mitigation**: Practical experience detecting and preventing algorithmic bias
- **Safety Mechanisms**: Implementing human-in-the-loop validation systems
- **Equity Focus**: Prioritising underserved populations in technology design

## 📚 Implementation Phases

### Phase 1: Research and Design (Weeks 1-3)
**Objective**: Understand the problem space and design culturally-sensitive solutions

#### Week 1: Problem Analysis
- Analyse global menopause education gaps
- Research cultural perspectives on menopause (focus on India)
- Evaluate existing solutions and identify failure points

#### Week 2: User Research
- Create diverse user personas representing different cultural contexts
- Design and deploy cultural assessment surveys
- Conduct primary research with target demographics
- Analyse patterns in cultural attitudes and information-seeking behaviours

#### Week 3: System Design
- Design cultural assessment modules
- Define content pathways for different cultural perspectives
- Plan safety mechanisms and bias prevention strategies
- Create a detailed system architecture with ethical considerations

### Phase 2: Technical Implementation (Weeks 4-7)
**Objective**: Build a functional prototype with safety and cultural adaptation features

#### Core System Components:
1. **Cultural Assessment Module**: Sophisticated screening to route users appropriately
2. **Content Pathways**: Liberation, Medical, and Neutral pathways adapting to worldviews
3. **Safety Validation Layer**: Multi-tier validation including medical accuracy checks
4. **Bias Detection System**: Automated detection of cultural and demographic biases
5. **LLM Integration**: Safe integration with modern language models

## 🛠️ Technical Architecture

### Enhanced Cultural Assessment System
The system now features a comprehensive 12-category cultural assessment framework:

1. **Life Transition Perspectives** - Understanding user's view on major life changes
2. **Menstruation and Reproductive Health Views** - Attitudes toward reproductive experiences
3. **Social and Family Context** - Community and family cultural dynamics
4. **Religious and Spiritual Beliefs** - Impact of spiritual beliefs on health views
5. **Health and Wellness Approach** - Preferred health management strategies
6. **Socioeconomic and Educational Context** - Educational and economic background factors
7. **Communication Preferences** - Language and information processing preferences
8. **Menopause Journey and Current Experience** - Personal menopause status and goals
9. **Healthcare and Support Systems** - Healthcare access and support preferences
10. **Learning Preferences and Information Processing** - Educational content preferences
11. **Technology and Digital Engagement** - Digital literacy and privacy concerns
12. **Work and Social Life Impact** - Professional and social considerations

### Dual-Pathway System
```
User Input → Cultural Assessment → Pathway Routing → Content Generation → Safety Validation → Response Delivery
```

**Cultural Pathways:**
- **Liberation Pathway**: For users viewing menopause as empowerment and natural transition
- **Loss/Medical Pathway**: For users experiencing menopause as medical challenge requiring symptom management
- **Neutral Pathway**: Balanced approach for users without strong cultural framing

### Integrated Safety Mechanisms
- **Multi-tier Cultural Assessment**: 12-category framework for accurate pathway determination
- **Persona-Driven Design**: Comprehensive user persona template for diverse user types
- **Progressive Technical Implementation**: Beginner to advanced notebook progression
- **Cultural Sensitivity Validation**: Built-in bias detection and cultural appropriateness checks
