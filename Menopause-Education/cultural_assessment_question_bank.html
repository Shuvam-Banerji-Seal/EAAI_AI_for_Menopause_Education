Implementation Notes:

  • Scoring System: Liberation-oriented responses suggest positive menopause framing; Loss-oriented responses suggest medical/symptomatic focus; Neutral responses indicate balanced approach needed.
  • Cultural Sensitivity: Questions avoid direct cultural stereotyping while gathering necessary pathway information.
  • Adaptive Questioning: AI system should adjust question sequence based on initial responses to avoid redundancy.
  • Privacy: All responses should be anonymized and encrypted according to the privacy framework outlined in the research paper.
  • Continuous Learning: User feedback should inform question bank refinement and cultural adaptation.

Pathway Determination Algorithm

Scoring Guidelines:

Liberation Pathway (Score ≥ 70%): User views menopause as freedom, values traditional wisdom, comes from cultures with elder respect, limited healthcare access.

Loss Pathway (Score ≥ 70%): User focuses on symptom management, concerns about physical changes, values medical intervention, Western cultural influence.

Neutral Pathway (Mixed Scores): Balanced perspectives, uncertain responses, or mixed cultural influences requiring comprehensive approach.

Dynamic Adaptation: Pathway can be adjusted based on ongoing interaction patterns and user feedback.
