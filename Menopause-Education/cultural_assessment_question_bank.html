Sample Cultural Assessment Question Bank

Culturally Sensitive Pathway Determination

Menopause Education AI Assignment

Summary

This assignment involves creating an AI-powered educational tool for menopause. It includes developing a cultural assessment question bank, a safety validation notebook, and user personas to guide the AI's development.

Topics

Applied AI, AI Ethics, Human-Computer Interaction, Natural Language Processing

Audience

Advanced AI, Undergraduate Research

Difficulty

Medium to Hard. Requires understanding of AI concepts, ethics, and some programming. Estimated 20-30 hours.

Strengths

Addresses a real-world problem with significant social impact. Encourages students to think about AI ethics and human-centered design. Provides a framework for developing AI for a sensitive topic.

Dependencies

Basic knowledge of Python and Jupyter Notebooks. Familiarity with AI concepts and machine learning. Access to a computer with a web browser and a Python environment.

🎯 Instructions for Implementation

Purpose: This question bank helps determine the appropriate cultural pathway (Liberation, Loss or Neutral) for personalised menopause education.

Scoring: Each response is coded to suggest pathway preference. The AI system should use weighted scoring rather than rigid categorisation.

Cultural Sensitivity: Questions are designed to respect diverse perspectives while gathering necessary information for personalisation.

🔧 Implementation Notes

  • Scoring System: Liberation oriented responses suggest positive menopause framing. Loss oriented responses suggest a medical/symptomatic focus. Neutral responses indicate a balanced approach is needed.
  • Cultural Sensitivity: Questions avoid direct cultural stereotyping while gathering necessary pathway information.
  • Adaptive Questioning: AI system should adjust the question sequence based on initial responses to avoid redundancy.
  • Privacy: All responses should be anonymised and encrypted according to the privacy framework outlined in the research paper.
  • Continuous Learning: User feedback should inform question bank refinement and cultural adaptation.
