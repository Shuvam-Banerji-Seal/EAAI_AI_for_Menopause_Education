Breaking the Silence: LLMs for Menopause Education in Culturally Diverse Contexts

Assignment Overview

Summary This assignment involves creating an AI-powered educational tool for menopause. It includes developing a cultural assessment question bank, a safety validation notebook, and user personas to guide the AI's development.
Topics Applied AI, AI Ethics, Human-Computer Interaction, Natural Language Processing
Audience Advanced AI, Undergraduate Research
Difficulty Medium to Hard. Requires understanding of AI concepts, ethics and some programming. Estimated 20-30 hours.
Strengths Addresses a real-world problem with significant social impact. Encourages students to think about AI ethics and human-centered design. Provides a framework for developing AI for a sensitive topic.
Challenges Requires a multidisciplinary approach that may be challenging for students with a purely technical background. The topic of menopause may be unfamiliar to some students.
Dependencies Basic knowledge of Python and Jupyter Notebooks. Familiarity with AI concepts and machine learning. Access to a computer with a web browser and a Python environment.
Extensions The assignment can be extended to include the development of a functional chatbot or a more comprehensive educational platform. Students could also be tasked with collecting and analyzing data from the cultural assessment question bank. The user personas can be expanded to include more diverse backgrounds.
